# Project image provenance

Photographs in `public/images/` for the land and agriculture listings. All are
**CC0 / public domain**, sourced through [Openverse](https://openverse.org)
(the search index run by WordPress). CC0 imposes no attribution requirement —
this file exists so a future maintainer knows where they came from and that
they are safe to keep, not because credit is owed.

They are stock placeholders, not photographs of the actual parcels. Replace
them with site photography before these listings are used in a sales context;
a stock beach standing in for a specific titled parcel is fine as decoration
and misleading as evidence.

Each is centre-cropped to 1600x1000 (16:10, matching the project card) and
saved as JPEG at quality 82.

## The Chukka Dome

`chukka-dome-1.jpg` and `chukka-dome-2.jpg` are crops of *Interier Planetum
Prague 2025 A / B* — **CC0**, from Wikimedia Commons, 4080x3072 originals:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Interier_Planetum_Prague_2025_A.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Interier_Planetum_Prague_2025_B.jpg

A landscape photograph said nothing about what this venue is, so these show
the actual format: a dome screen wrapping the audience above tiered seating.

Two things to be clear about. This is a **planetarium, not a Cosm venue** —
Cosm's own photographs are their copyrighted brand assets and are not usable
here, and neither is their name as a claim of involvement. And this is not a
render of the Chukka Dome design; it stands in for the venue type until one
exists. The crops deliberately exclude the Prague venue's on-screen wordmark
and signage, so no other operator's branding appears on a FIBI listing.

The remaining Cianda listings reuse existing `hero*` images as placeholders;
those should be replaced with renders from the Artis master plan.
